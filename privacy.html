Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Portal is a pre-launch concept for hospital shift coverage, run by its founder in Florida. This page explains what we collect on this website and why. It covers this website and our emails only; Portal does not collect any patient information.

What we collect

  • What you type in the signup form: name, work email, role, hospital and bed range.
  • How the site is used: pages viewed, buttons clicked, the link that brought you here (campaign tags), approximate location from your IP address, and browser and device type. We record anonymized session replays to see where the page is confusing; everything typed into form fields is masked and never recorded.
  • Email engagement: whether our welcome email was opened and whether its link was clicked.
  • Anything you choose to send us, such as feedback or replies to our emails.

Why we use it

  • To send you the welcome email and up to two follow-ups about the founding-facility program.
  • To contact you about conversations, design previews and pilot access.
  • To understand which messages and pricing ideas are useful, so we can decide what to build.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Services that process it for us

  • PostHog (United States) for site analytics, experiments, surveys and session replay.
  • Google (Gmail / Google Sheets) to store your contact details and send our emails.
  • Vercel to host this website.

How long we keep it

We keep signup details while Portal is in development and for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Analytics data is kept according to PostHog's retention settings for our project.

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply "remove me" at any time.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold about you by replying to any of our emails.
  • You can block analytics with your browser's tracking protection; the page still works.

Contact

Reply to any Portal email, or write to the address the welcome email was sent from.
