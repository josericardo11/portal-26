What we're designing

Post a shift. Watch it fill.

  1. Post the open shift with unit, time and required credentials.
  2. Qualified nurses in your own network (staff, PRN, float pool) see it on their phone and claim it.
  3. You see live coverage on one board, with every claim approved by your staffing office.

This is the design direction, not a finished product. Founding facilities decide what goes in the pilot.
